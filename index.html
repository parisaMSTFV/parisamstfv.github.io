02 / APPROACH

What makes an analysis useful?

Technical rigor matters. But an analysis is only useful when it clarifies a choice, makes uncertainty explicit, and can be acted on.

01

Start with the business decision

Define the choice, the available actions, and the KPI before choosing a method.

02

Challenge the easy explanation

Use baselines, careful validation, holdouts, and causal designs where appropriate. Treat estimates as estimates.

03

Connect the result to action

Specify constraints, economics, trade-offs, and what to measure after the decision is implemented.
